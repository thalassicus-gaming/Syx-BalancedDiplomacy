The Balanced Diplomacy mod fixes a number of problems in the vanilla game.

**Peace deals were unwinnable.** A faction on the verge of destruction could demand you hand over half your empire, and no code path let a defeated enemy pay *you*. Terms now come from relative army strength and how long the war has run, with the loser paying up to 10% of what they can offer — and a beaten faction can be made to pay tribute.

**Peace demands ignored the war.** The size came from your wealth and a global exposure figure identical for every faction; a single enemy stack near your capital maxed out every peace demand on the map. Demands now depend on the specific faction across the table — their army against yours, and how long you've been fighting them.

**Instant-peace exploitation.** Nothing stopped you declaring war on a hopeless target and immediately extracting tribute. Every faction now opens a war blustering, and that only wears off as the war drags on, so you have to actually prosecute it to collect.

**Agreement-saving demands were impossible by construction.** When the game couldn't assemble enough goods, it wrote the shortfall straight into your treasury total, then refused to let you accept what it had just invented. Offers are now built only from things you own, and partial payment earns proportional goodwill.

**Random threats demanded 20–40% of your entire realm** including territory, with no military consideration, and refusing carried a heavy penalty that triggered more threats. Now measured against what you can actually part with, land is off the table, and the size scales with relative army strength.

**Gifts were pointless.** Your entire treasury moved a mid-size faction by 0.04 opinion when 1.5 was needed for a trade agreement — and the game forgets small gifts fastest, so every gift you could afford was in the category it discards. Gifts are now worth roughly twenty times more and persist for years.

**Weak factions harassed you constantly.** Rivalry rose with your wealth, so the poorest neighbours were always the most hostile — extortion every few days, and punishing events lasting longer than the gap between threats. Hostility now peaks between comparable realms and collapses against factions that can't field an army.

**Players razed conquered cities to avoid neighbours.** Since only bordering factions can interact and every bordering faction was hostile, destroying your own gains was rational. Expanding now changes *who* your rivals are rather than how many.

**Vassals were unusable.** A new vassal had no wealth and no army, which maxed rivalry and caused instant hostility. A faction with no army is now no rival at all.

**King's pride was invisible randomness**, swinging gift memory 2.4x and demand sizes up to 4x on a trait you can't see or influence. Capped at 25%.
